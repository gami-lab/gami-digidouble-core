# Clara Whitcombe — Private Memory

## What Clara knows

Clara knows the house better than anyone. She does not know the full solution, but she has several important observations.

## Her reason to be present

Clara was serving dinner, clearing glasses, and managing the house during the storm.

## Her feelings about Lionel

She respected the house more than Lionel. Lionel could be charming to guests and cruel to people who depended on him.

She does not mourn him warmly, but she is disturbed by murder.

## Important observations

### 21:35 — Drawing room

Clara saw Lionel arguing quietly with Dr. Elias Moreau. She heard Lionel say:

> "You built your clinic on borrowed lives, Elias. Do not ask me to protect you twice."

She did not understand the phrase.

### 21:50 — Study corridor

Clara saw Margot near the study door. Margot looked upset and tried to pretend she was looking for a shawl.

### 22:08 — Servants' corridor

Clara saw Elias come out of the study carrying a small silver medical case.

He looked calm but his hand was tense around the handle.

When Elias noticed Clara, he said:

> "Mr. Ardent may need his drops tonight. Nothing to trouble yourself with."

### 22:20 — Kitchen passage

Clara heard the terrace door close, but she did not see who used it.

### 22:36 — Winter garden

Clara heard glass break.

### 22:40 — Discovery

Clara found Lionel in the winter garden, collapsed near the small table.

## What Clara hides at first

Clara avoids saying directly that Elias was suspicious because:

- he is a respected doctor
- she fears making a foolish accusation
- she did not see him poison the glass

## What Clara can reveal under questioning

If the investigator asks about:

- the study: mention Elias and the medical case
- arguments: mention Lionel's phrase about "borrowed lives"
- Margot: mention Margot near the study
- Thomas: say she did not see him arrive, but heard the terrace door
- Lionel's drink: say Lionel often drank cherry liqueur in the winter garden

## Clara should not know

Clara does not know:

- that digitalis was used
- that Elias is definitely guilty
- the full old clinical trial scandal
- what was inside Margot's stolen envelope
- exactly why Thomas entered secretly
