# Shared Clues

These clues may be retrieved by any avatar.

## Publicly known facts

- Lionel Ardent had a heart condition.
- Lionel drank cherry liqueur regularly.
- Lionel was found dead in the winter garden.
- A storm delayed police arrival.
- Four people were in or near the villa that evening:
  - Clara Whitcombe
  - Dr. Elias Moreau
  - Margot Vale
  - Thomas Reed
- Lionel had a reputation for manipulating people with money and secrets.

## Weak clues

These create suspicion but do not solve the case alone.

- Margot needed money.
- Thomas entered without being invited.
- Clara knows the house and had access to rooms.
- Elias was the physician and controlled the medical interpretation.

## Strong clues

These should become important during the investigation.

- Elias had medical access.
- Elias was too quick to declare heart attack.
- Lionel had threatened Elias.
- Clara saw Elias with a medical case near the study.
- Thomas saw Elias near the terrace around the time Elias claimed to be in the library.
- Lionel carried a glass to the winter garden shortly before collapsing.

## Contradictions

### Elias timeline contradiction

Elias says he was in the library between 22:05 and 22:30.

Thomas says Elias was near the terrace / winter garden around 22:20.

Both cannot be true.

### Margot false lead

Margot stole an envelope, but theft after the death is not proof of murder.

### Thomas false lead

Thomas entered secretly, but his secret entry explains the footprints without explaining the poison.

## Good questions for the user to ask

- Who touched Lionel's drink?
- Who had access to medical substances?
- Why was Elias so sure it was a heart attack?
- What was Lionel planning to reveal?
- Where was each suspect between 22:05 and 22:30?
- What did Clara see from the servants' corridor?
- What could Thomas see from the library?
- Why did Margot go into the study?
