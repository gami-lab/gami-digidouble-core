# GM Truth — Full Solution

This document is for the Game Master / operator only.

It must not be visible to avatars.

## The murderer

Dr. Elias Moreau killed Lionel Ardent.

## Motive

Twenty years earlier, Elias participated in a private clinical trial funded by Lionel. The trial caused severe harm to several patients. Elias falsified parts of the report to protect his career. Lionel kept copies of the documents.

On the night of the murder, Lionel told Elias he planned to send the documents to a journalist and remove Elias from his will.

Elias believed his career, clinic, reputation, and public identity would be destroyed.

## Method

Elias carried a small vial of concentrated digitalis solution in his medical case.

At approximately 22:12, Elias entered Lionel's study under the pretext of looking for Lionel's heart medication. He retrieved the vial from a hidden compartment in his medical case.

At approximately 22:18, Elias passed through the drawing room, where Lionel's cherry liqueur glass was temporarily unattended. He added digitalis to the glass.

At approximately 22:25, Lionel took the glass to the winter garden.

At approximately 22:36, Lionel collapsed.

At approximately 22:40, Clara found the body.

## Elias's lie

Elias claims he was in the library reading medical correspondence between 22:05 and 22:30.

This is false.

## Evidence against Elias

Strong evidence:

- Clara saw Elias near the study around 22:08 carrying a silver medical case.
- Thomas saw Elias near the terrace door around 22:20, close to the winter garden.
- Elias had access to digitalis.
- Lionel threatened Elias earlier that evening.
- Elias was too quick to declare a heart attack.
- A torn medical report in Lionel's study refers to the old trial.
- Elias tries to dismiss poison before anyone suggests a specific poison.

## False leads

### Margot

Margot stole an envelope from Lionel's study after the body was discovered. The envelope contained a letter proving Lionel planned to disinherit her. She feared scandal and poverty. She did not kill Lionel.

### Thomas

Thomas entered the villa secretly through the terrace and hid in the library. He wanted documents for an article about Lionel. He did not poison Lionel. His muddy footprints create suspicion.

### Clara

Clara knows more than she first says because she is afraid of accusing a respected doctor. She did not kill Lionel.

## Confession rule

Elias should not confess immediately.

He may confess only when confronted with a tight chain of evidence, for example:

- poison was digitalis
- Elias had access to it
- Clara saw him with the medical case near the study
- Thomas saw him near the winter garden
- Lionel was about to expose the old clinical trial

If the user simply asks "Are you guilty?", Elias denies it.

If the user confronts him with partial evidence, he redirects suspicion toward Margot or Thomas.

If the user confronts him with the full chain, he may break, but with dignity and bitterness rather than melodrama.

## GM progression hints

If the user is stuck:

- suggest comparing timelines
- suggest asking about the drink
- suggest asking who had medical knowledge
- suggest asking why Elias declared heart attack so quickly
- suggest asking Thomas what he saw from the library
- suggest asking Clara what she saw in the servants' corridor

Do not reveal the solution directly.
