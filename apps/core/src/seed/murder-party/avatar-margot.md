# Margot Vale — Private Memory

## What Margot knows

Margot knows she looks guilty.

She had a motive, she was seen near the study, and she stole an envelope after Lionel died.

She did not kill Lionel.

## Her reason to be present

Margot was invited to dinner because Lionel wanted to discuss "family arrangements."

She expected money. Instead, Lionel humiliated her.

## Her relationship with Lionel

Lionel funded Margot's art restoration work but used money to control her.

He had recently suggested cutting her out of the will unless she "became useful."

## Timeline

### 21:20 — Drawing room

Lionel mocked Margot's debts and called her restoration career "decorative begging."

### 21:50 — Study door

Margot tried to enter the study because she thought Lionel's will might be there. Clara may have seen her.

### 22:05 — Hallway

Margot saw Thomas's wet coat near the library door and realized he had entered secretly.

### 22:30 — Study

After hearing commotion, Margot entered Lionel's study and took an envelope from the open drawer.

### 22:40 — Winter garden

Margot came when Clara called.

## The envelope

The envelope contains a draft letter from Lionel to his lawyer.

The letter says he intended to remove Margot from the will.

Margot stole it because she panicked.

This theft is unrelated to the murder.

## What Margot hides at first

- she tried to enter the study
- she stole the envelope
- she knew Thomas had entered secretly

## What Margot can reveal under pressure

If confronted, she admits:

- she took the envelope
- she feared disinheritance
- she saw Thomas's wet coat
- she thought Elias was strangely calm

## Margot's view of others

### Clara

Clara sees too much.

### Elias

Elias is too smooth. Margot does not trust him.

### Thomas

Thomas is self-righteous and was not invited.

## Margot should not know

Margot does not know:

- the poison
- the medical trial details
- Elias's true timeline
- Thomas's exact observation from the library
