# Places — Villa Miralac

## Villa Miralac

An old lakeside villa near Lausanne. The house is elegant but slightly decayed. It has long corridors, tall windows, creaking floors, and too many doors for a modern building.

A storm has cut the road and made the garden paths muddy.

## Drawing Room

The evening began here.

Important elements:

- fireplace
- drinks tray
- cherry liqueur decanter
- four used glasses
- Lionel's favorite armchair
- a side table with an empty cigar case

Most guests were seen here between 21:00 and 21:45.

## Winter Garden

Lionel was found here at approximately 22:40.

Important elements:

- glass roof struck by rain
- orange trees in large ceramic pots
- one broken liqueur glass near the tiled steps
- a chair knocked slightly backward
- traces of spilled cherry liqueur
- damp footprints entering from the terrace door
- a strong smell of wet leaves and alcohol

This is the crime scene.

## Study

Lionel's private room.

Important elements:

- locked writing desk
- opened drawer
- torn corner of a medical report
- missing envelope
- small burn mark in the fireplace
- a silver letter opener
- cabinet containing old correspondence

Lionel had been preparing to change his will and expose old secrets.

## Library

A smaller room connected to the study by a side door.

Important elements:

- shelves of old books
- dark green curtains
- a window facing the terrace
- a narrow place where someone could hide
- a view toward the winter garden

Thomas hid here for part of the evening.

## Terrace

The terrace faces the lake.

Important elements:

- wet stone floor
- muddy footprints
- access to the winter garden
- access to the library window
- a garden path leading to the boathouse

Thomas entered the house this way. Elias also used the terrace later.

## Servants' Corridor

A narrow corridor behind the kitchen and study.

Important elements:

- poor lighting
- service door near the study
- old bell system
- view toward the study entrance

Clara saw Elias here around 22:08.

## Boathouse

Small building near the water.

Important elements:

- old rowing boat
- oil lamp
- damp rope
- locked cabinet
- a broken oar

This place is not the murder site, but it can be used to test additional location retrieval and false leads.

Margot briefly went near the boathouse earlier in the evening to hide a personal letter she did not want Lionel to find.
