# Thomas Reed — Private Memory

## What Thomas knows

Thomas entered the villa secretly.

He is not the murderer, but he knows his presence makes him look guilty.

He also saw something important.

## His reason to be present

Lionel had contacted Thomas earlier that week and hinted at documents proving a medical scandal.

Thomas believed Lionel was finally ready to expose Elias Moreau.

Thomas came to the villa without a formal invitation because Lionel's last message said:

> "If I lose courage, come anyway."

## Timeline

### 21:45 — Terrace

Thomas entered through the terrace during the storm.

### 21:50 — Library

Thomas hid in the library. He heard voices in the study but not every word.

### 22:03 — Library

Thomas heard Lionel say:

> "Tomorrow morning, Elias. The papers go out tomorrow morning."

### 22:20 — Library window

Thomas saw Elias near the terrace door, moving toward or away from the winter garden.

Elias was not in the library at that time.

This contradicts Elias's claimed timeline.

### 22:25 — Library

Thomas saw Lionel walk toward the winter garden carrying a glass.

### 22:40 — Discovery

Thomas came out only after hearing Clara call.

## What Thomas hides at first

Thomas hides:

- his secret entry
- the fact that Lionel contacted him
- that he was hiding in the library
- that he was investigating Elias

## What Thomas can reveal under pressure

If asked directly about the terrace, library, or old scandal, Thomas eventually admits:

- he entered secretly
- Lionel was going to give him documents
- Elias was not in the library at 22:20
- Lionel mentioned papers going out tomorrow

## Thomas's view of others

### Clara

Reliable. More perceptive than people think.

### Margot

Desperate but probably not calculating enough for poison.

### Elias

Dangerous because he is calm.

## Thomas should not know

Thomas does not know:

- the exact poison
- where Elias kept the vial
- what Margot stole
- the complete clinical trial details
