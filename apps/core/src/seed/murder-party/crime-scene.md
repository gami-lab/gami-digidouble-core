# Crime Scene — Winter Garden

## Discovery

Lionel Ardent was found dead in the winter garden at approximately 22:40.

Clara discovered the body after hearing glass break. She called the others. The storm delayed the arrival of the police.

## Body position

Lionel was seated awkwardly beside a small table, one hand near his chest, the other near the broken glass.

No visible wound.

## Objects near the body

- broken liqueur glass
- spilled cherry liqueur
- damp footprints near the terrace entrance
- one chair pulled out
- Lionel's handkerchief
- a cigar ash mark
- a faint bitter almond-like note mixed with the liqueur smell, but this is misleading and not the poison

## Medical clue

Lionel had heart problems. A natural heart attack is plausible at first.

However:

- the sudden collapse happened shortly after drinking
- Lionel's pulse and symptoms were consistent with digitalis poisoning
- Dr. Elias Moreau was too quick to call it a heart attack
- Lionel's medication history does not explain the dose required

## Broken glass

The glass likely broke when Lionel collapsed.

The poison was already in the drink before the glass broke.

## The drink

Lionel drank cherry liqueur after dinner. He often drank it in the winter garden.

Important:

- the decanter was in the drawing room
- Lionel's personal glass was brought to the winter garden
- Elias had a private moment near the drinks tray and later near Lionel

## Footprints

There are damp footprints near the terrace door.

These can implicate Thomas, who entered through the terrace earlier.

But they do not prove he poisoned Lionel.

## Real mechanism

The murderer added concentrated digitalis solution to Lionel's cherry liqueur.

The poison came from Elias's medical case.

## Evidence needed to solve

The case should be solvable through the combination of:

1. poison clue
2. Elias's medical access
3. Elias's motive
4. contradiction in Elias's movements
5. witness statements from Clara and Thomas
